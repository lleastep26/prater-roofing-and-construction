Prater Roofing and Construction — Roofing Services in Newcastle

Prater Roofing and Construction provides comprehensive roofing services in Newcastle and the surrounding metro area. With eight years of local experience, we handle roof replacements, repairs, inspections and storm response across OKC, Norman, Moore, Mustang and Edmond.
